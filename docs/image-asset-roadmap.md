# Eastern Mysticism Archive Image Asset Roadmap

## 1. Purpose

This roadmap is the master planning document for every future visual asset in Eastern Mysticism Archive.

Its purpose is to track planned images systematically so production does not become ad hoc. Every image should support the archive's educational mission, strengthen topical authority, and become part of a reusable long-term visual knowledge base.

The roadmap should help future editors, designers, and AI agents answer:

- Which pages need images first?
- What type of image does each page need?
- Which diagrams can be reused across multiple articles?
- Which assets support SEO, Google Images, Pinterest, Open Graph, books, courses, and video?
- How can the archive scale visually from 200 pages to 1000+ pages without losing consistency?

The goal is not to create images for every page immediately. The goal is to prioritize the most valuable images first and build a reusable visual system over time.

## 2. Priority Levels

### Priority A: Authority Pages

Authority pages are the highest-value evergreen pages in the archive. They explain foundational concepts such as Dao, Qi, Yin Yang, Five Elements, Bagua, Taiji, Lo Shu, Luopan, and Taoist cosmology.

Production order: These pages should receive hero images, knowledge diagrams, and Open Graph assets before any lower-priority work.

### Priority B: Cluster Hub Pages

Cluster hubs organize major topic areas such as I Ching, Chinese Zodiac, Talisman, Sacred Animals, Eight Immortals, Chinese Deities, Festival Calendar, and Cosmology.

Production order: These pages should receive hero images and relationship maps after Priority A pages are covered.

### Priority C: Important Supporting Pages

Important supporting pages explain entities, subtopics, rituals, symbols, timelines, or relationships that strengthen a hub. They may not require full image sets, but they often need diagrams, comparison images, or symbolic illustrations.

Production order: These pages should be handled after core hubs have visual coverage.

### Priority D: Long-tail Pages

Long-tail pages support depth across the archive. They may receive thumbnails, small illustrations, icons, or reused global diagrams rather than custom full-scale visuals.

Production order: These pages should be produced last or when a reusable template can cover many related pages efficiently.

## 10. Production Phases

### Phase 1: Authority Pages

Create core visuals for the highest-value evergreen pages. Focus on Dao, Qi, Wu Wei, Yin Yang, Taiji Diagram, Five Elements, Bagua, Lo Shu Square, Luopan, Qi in Feng Shui, Five Elements and Bagua, and Cosmology in Taoism.

Primary asset types:

- Hero images
- Knowledge diagrams
- Open Graph images
- Pinterest versions for the strongest search topics

### Phase 2: Cluster Pages

Create visuals for cluster hubs and topic networks.

Primary asset types:

- Cluster hero images
- Relationship maps
- Knowledge diagrams
- Cluster timelines where relevant

### Phase 3: Supporting Pages

Create selective visuals for important supporting pages that explain complex subtopics.

Primary asset types:

- Symbol illustrations
- Comparison diagrams
- Small knowledge diagrams
- Reused global diagrams

### Phase 4: Pinterest Assets

Create vertical discovery graphics for evergreen search topics.

Primary asset types:

- Vertical diagrams
- Concept summaries
- Festival and zodiac guides
- Symbol explanation graphics

### Phase 5: Books

Adapt the strongest visual assets for future books, PDFs, and print-style educational guides.

Primary asset types:

- Chapter opener illustrations
- Black-and-white compatible versions
- Print-safe timeline graphics

### Phase 6: Courses

Adapt visual assets for structured learning experiences.

Primary asset types:

- Lesson diagrams
- Slide visuals
- Concept maps
- Quiz-support illustrations
- Sequential learning graphics

### Phase 7: Video Assets

Adapt diagrams into motion-ready educational assets.

Primary asset types:

- Layered diagrams
- Animated flow maps
- Timeline sequences
- Icon sets
- Intro and title cards

## 11. Long-term Vision

This roadmap allows Eastern Mysticism Archive to evolve into a complete educational visual knowledge base rather than only a text website.

At maturity, the archive should contain a coherent library of manuscript-style educational visuals that help readers understand complex traditions quickly and accurately. A reader should be able to move from a text article to a diagram, from a diagram to a related map, from a map to a timeline, and from a timeline to a cluster relationship view.

The long-term goal is visual continuity across the whole knowledge graph:

- Authority pages explain foundational systems.
- Cluster hubs show relationships among entities.
- Diagrams clarify abstract concepts.
- Timelines show historical and seasonal development.
- Maps show spatial and cosmological structure.
- Icons provide fast recognition across categories.
- Pinterest and Open Graph versions extend discovery beyond the website.

With this roadmap, future visual production can become predictable, reusable, and scalable. Each new image should strengthen not only one page, but the archive's entire educational ecosystem.
