# Star Pages Strategy

**Project:** Eastern Mysticism Archive  
**Date:** 2026-07-24  
**KAPF stage:** Performance Validation

## Purpose

Star Pages are pages and topic groups that Google Search Console is already testing through search impressions. They receive optimization priority because current performance data shows that Google is evaluating their relevance, authority, and usefulness.

The strategy directs effort toward demonstrated opportunities rather than speculative expansion. A Star Page should be strengthened through deeper coverage, clearer relationships, better visuals, and more useful internal navigation before the project creates unrelated content.

Star Page status is a prioritization signal, not a permanent label. Tiers should be reviewed against current Search Console data, query quality, ranking movement, click potential, and supporting-cluster strength.

## Tier A (★★★★★)

- Yin Yang
- Yin Yang Symbol
- Yin Yang Meaning

### Optimization Priorities

- Expand article depth.
- Add accurate educational diagrams.
- Add consistent archive-style illustrations.
- Improve FAQ coverage using real search intent.
- Strengthen contextual internal links.
- Add comparison sections for commonly confused concepts.
- Add related concepts that improve semantic completeness.

### Objective

Build a comprehensive Yin Yang authority system in which the main concept, symbol, meaning, cosmological context, and related entities support one another. Improvements should increase user understanding while giving Google a clear topic structure.

## Tier B (★★★★☆)

- Eight Immortals
- Taoist Gods
- Taoist Deities
- Five Elements
- Lo Shu
- Ancestor Worship
- Ancestor Hall
- Taoist Cosmology

### Optimization Priorities

- Expand and improve supporting pages.
- Strengthen topical authority around each hub.
- Improve semantic coverage of important subtopics and relationships.
- Increase useful contextual internal links within and between clusters.

### Objective

Turn emerging search visibility into durable cluster authority. Each hub should provide a complete overview, connect to strong supporting entities, answer relevant questions, and bridge naturally to adjacent established topics.

## Tier C

Tier C is the intake queue for future pages that begin receiving meaningful Google impressions.

A page should automatically enter Tier C when Search Console shows that Google is testing it for relevant queries, even if clicks or rankings remain limited. Entry should trigger a review of:

- Query relevance and search intent.
- Current article quality and completeness.
- Internal inbound and outbound links.
- Supporting-page coverage.
- Potential diagrams, comparisons, or FAQs.
- Overlap with an existing Star Page or topic cluster.

Tier C pages should be monitored before major expansion. Pages developing stronger, consistent signals may move to Tier B or Tier A. Pages receiving irrelevant impressions should be corrected or deprioritized rather than expanded automatically.

## Review Cadence

Review Star Page tiers at least monthly during Performance Validation. Record material changes in impressions, queries, average position, clicks, click-through rate, and cluster behavior.

Search Console evidence should guide movement between tiers. Optimization should remain focused on user value, accurate knowledge, and long-term authority rather than short-term ranking reactions.
