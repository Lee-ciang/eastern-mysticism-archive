# Overview

Dragon symbolism is one of the most influential animal-symbol systems in Chinese culture.

Dragons appear in mythology, imperial imagery, festivals, architecture, zodiac traditions, water symbolism, and cosmology.

This page treats dragons as cultural and mythological symbols.

It does not present dragons as factual animals or sources of supernatural power.

# Water and Weather

Chinese dragons are often associated with water, clouds, rain, rivers, seas, and seasonal vitality.

These meanings connect dragon imagery with agriculture and natural cycles.

# Authority and Cultural Identity

Dragon imagery has also been associated with imperial authority and cultural identity.

Its meaning changes across historical and artistic settings.
