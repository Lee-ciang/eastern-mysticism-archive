# Overview

The Qilin is an auspicious mythological creature in Chinese culture.

It may be represented with features associated with deer, horses, dragons, scales, horns, or hooves.

Its form varies across periods and artistic traditions.

This archive treats the Qilin as cultural symbolism rather than a factual animal.

# Auspicious Meaning

The Qilin is often associated with virtue, peace, wise rule, exceptional births, and benevolent order.

Its appearance in stories may signal an important moral or historical event.

# Composite Form

Qilin imagery combines features from several animals.

This composite form expresses symbolic richness rather than biological description.
