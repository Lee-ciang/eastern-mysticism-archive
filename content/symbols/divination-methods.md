# Overview

Divination methods are the procedures used to generate an I Ching reading. They provide a way to move from a question or situation to a symbolic pattern, usually a hexagram and sometimes changing lines. The method gives the consultation structure and ritual seriousness.

This page introduces the main methods used in the I Ching / Divination Cluster and links them to `i-ching-divination`, `hexagrams`, `changing-lines`, `casting-coins`, and `yarrow-stalk-method`.

# Method and Meaning

In I Ching divination, a method is not just a randomizing tool. It frames the act of consultation. The questioner pauses, formulates a concern, performs the method, and receives a symbolic pattern. Interpretation then depends on the resulting lines, trigrams, hexagrams, and textual tradition.

This process belongs to a wider set of `oracle-traditions`, where signs are treated as meaningful within a ritual or symbolic context.

# Coin Casting

`casting-coins` is one of the best-known modern I Ching methods. It commonly uses three coins tossed six times to form the six lines of a hexagram. The combinations can produce stable Yin, stable Yang, changing Yin, or changing Yang lines.

Coin casting is simpler and faster than the yarrow stalk method, which helped make it widely used.

# Yarrow Stalk Method

The `yarrow-stalk-method` is a traditional method associated with a more elaborate counting procedure. It takes longer and is often treated as more ceremonial. The method generates line values through repeated divisions and counts.

Because of its slower pace, the yarrow stalk method can give the consultation a contemplative rhythm.

# Hexagrams, Trigrams, and Changing Lines

All methods eventually produce a pattern that can be interpreted through `hexagrams`, `trigrams`, and `changing-lines`. The hexagram is the main figure, the trigrams provide internal symbolic structure, and changing lines show transformation.

This is why method and interpretation cannot be fully separated. The method generates the structure that interpretation reads.
