# Overview

The Vermilion Bird is a sacred directional beast associated with the south in Chinese cosmology.

It is known as Zhuque.

The bird is linked with summer, fire, red color symbolism, and celestial order.

This archive treats the Vermilion Bird as cultural symbolism.
