# Overview

Ritual symbols in Taoism include talismans, seals, diagrams, deity names, directional signs, ritual implements, colors, cosmological references, and written forms used in liturgical or protective contexts. These symbols appear in temple rituals, household practices, talismanic writing, purification rites, and boundary-marking customs.

This page functions as a conceptual bridge for the Talisman / Fu Symbol Cluster. It explains how Fu talismans fit into a wider ritual-symbolic language.

# Talismans as Ritual Symbols

`fu-talismans` are among the clearest examples of Taoist ritual symbolism. They combine writing, gesture, material, authority, and cosmology. Related pages such as `talisman-writing`, `talisman-paper`, `talisman-seals`, and `protective-fu-symbols` explain their component parts.

A talisman is not only a decorative object. It is a symbolic form embedded in a ritual system.
