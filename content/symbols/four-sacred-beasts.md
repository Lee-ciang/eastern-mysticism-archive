# Overview

The Four Sacred Beasts are a cosmological set of directional creatures in Chinese tradition.

They are the Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, and Black Tortoise.

The system connects animals with directions, seasons, colors, and phases.

This archive treats the Four Beasts as symbolic cosmology.

# Four Core Creatures

This page connects strongly with `azure-dragon-symbol`, `white-tiger-symbol`, `vermilion-bird-symbol`, and `black-tortoise-symbol`.

Each page explains one directional creature.

# Azure Dragon

The Azure Dragon is associated with the east and spring.

It may correspond with Wood in Five Elements symbolism.

# White Tiger

The White Tiger is associated with the west and autumn.

It may correspond with Metal.

# Vermilion Bird

The Vermilion Bird is associated with the south and summer.

It may correspond with Fire.

# Black Tortoise

The Black Tortoise is associated with the north and winter.

It may correspond with Water.
