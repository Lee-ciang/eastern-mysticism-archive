# Overview

Five Elements Symbolism refers to the meanings associated with Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water in Chinese cosmology. The system is more accurately called Five Phases, because it describes processes, movements, and relationships rather than fixed substances.

The Five Elements are among the most important symbolic systems in the archive. They connect cosmology, Feng Shui, Taoist interpretation, qi, Bagua, astrology, ritual thought, and seasonal cycles.

# Historical Context

Wu Xing developed as part of Chinese correlative thought. It organized many fields of meaning: seasons, directions, colors, sounds, bodily concepts, government, ritual, time, and spatial interpretation.

This symbolic system became influential because it could connect many areas of life through a shared pattern of relationships.

# The Five Symbols

Wood symbolizes growth, expansion, spring, and branching movement. Fire symbolizes heat, brightness, visibility, and active transformation. Earth symbolizes stability, nourishment, center, and mediation.

Metal symbolizes refinement, structure, contraction, and clarity. Water symbolizes depth, flow, storage, descent, and adaptability.

These meanings are interpretive categories rather than universal facts.

# Generating and Overcoming

Five Elements symbolism depends heavily on relationship. The generating cycle describes how phases support one another. The overcoming cycle describes how phases regulate one another.

Without these cycles, the Five Elements can be misunderstood as a simple list. Their meaning comes from movement.

# Relationship to Qi

Qi can be interpreted through Five Elements symbolism. Each phase describes a different quality of qi. Wood-like qi may expand, Fire-like qi may rise, Earth-like qi may stabilize, Metal-like qi may contract, and Water-like qi may descend or flow.

This relationship connects the Five Elements cluster to the Qi cluster.

# Relationship to Bagua

The Five Elements are often layered with Bagua and directional systems. A trigram, direction, or Bagua zone may be interpreted through elemental associations depending on the school.

This link is especially important in Feng Shui.

# Feng Shui Context

In Feng Shui, Five Elements symbolism may be applied to color, material, shape, direction, room quality, or landscape form. The archive presents these as cultural interpretations, not guaranteed effects.
