# Overview

The Azure Dragon is a sacred directional beast associated with the east in Chinese cosmology.

It is known as Qinglong.

The creature is linked with spring, growth, eastern direction, and dragon symbolism.

This archive treats the Azure Dragon as cultural and cosmological imagery.

The Azure Dragon is commonly associated with the east.

This role belongs to a symbolic map of directions and seasons.
