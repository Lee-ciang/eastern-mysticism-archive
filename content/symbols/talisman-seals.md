# Overview

Talisman seals are stamps, seal-like marks, or graphic signs used in talismanic and Taoist ritual contexts. They may appear on Fu talismans, ritual documents, protective writings, altar materials, or symbolic objects. A seal can mark authority, authentication, divine command, ritual closure, or sacred presence.

This archive treats talisman seals as cultural and historical symbols. It does not claim that seals possess factual supernatural power.

# Seal and Authority

In ordinary administration, a seal can mark permission, identity, and official authority. In religious imagination, similar logic may be applied to sacred or ritual authority. A talisman seal may indicate that a writing belongs to a recognized ritual order.

This connects `talisman-seals` to `talisman-writing`, `fu-talismans`, `ritual-symbols-in-taoism`, and `taoist-talisman-rituals`.

# Placement on Talismans

Seals may be placed on `talisman-paper` or combined with written marks and stylized script. They can appear as red stamps, black marks, hand-drawn signs, or seal-like forms depending on tradition.

In some contexts, they reinforce a talisman's role in `protective-talismans`, `protective-fu-symbols`, `protection-ritual`, and `protective-charms`.
