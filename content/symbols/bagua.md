# Overview

The Bagua is a symbolic system used in Taoist cosmology and the I Ching. It consists of eight trigrams representing natural forces, directions, and energetic principles.

# Historical Origins

The Bagua system is traditionally associated with the ancient Chinese text known as the I Ching, or Book of Changes.

# Symbolic Structure

Each trigram consists of broken and unbroken lines representing Yin and Yang energies.

# Feng Shui Connection

The Bagua is widely used in feng shui practices to map energetic zones within spaces and environments.

# Cultural Influence

The Bagua remains one of the most influential symbolic systems in East Asian philosophy and metaphysical traditions.
