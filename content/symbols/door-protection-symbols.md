# Overview

Door protection symbols are images, signs, inscriptions, and ritual objects placed around entrances in Chinese folk religion and household protective practice. They may include door gods, talismans, red paper, auspicious characters, Bagua mirrors, guardian images, or paired protective figures.

Doors are symbolically important because they mark the boundary between inside and outside. Protective symbols at a doorway express concern with safety, order, fortune, and the control of passage.

# Historical Context

Door protection practices developed through folk religion, temple imagery, New Year customs, Taoist talismanic traditions, and household ritual. Entrances have long been treated as vulnerable and meaningful places in many cultures. In Chinese traditions, they became a major site for protective imagery.

During Lunar New Year, door images and inscriptions may be renewed as part of household cleansing and seasonal renewal.

# Door Gods

Door gods are among the most recognizable door protection symbols. They are often shown as paired guardians, military figures, or divine protectors. Their placement on opposite sides of a door creates a balanced protective frame.

The archive treats this as traditional protective symbolism rather than evidence of literal guardianship.

# Talismans and Inscriptions

Talismans or red paper inscriptions may also be placed at doors. These can include written blessings, protective phrases, ritual marks, or auspicious characters.

The visual presence of writing marks the doorway as ritually and culturally significant.

# Bagua and Feng Shui

In some contexts, Bagua diagrams or Bagua mirrors are associated with doorway protection. These objects connect the entrance with Feng Shui, directional symbolism, and qi flow.

Such uses vary by tradition and should not be treated as universal practice.

# Threshold Meaning

Door protection symbols belong to a larger system of threshold rituals. The doorway is where guests enter, influences pass, and household space begins. Symbolic protection at this point expresses the desire to maintain order within the home.

# Cultural Significance

Door protection symbols connect door gods, protective talismans, threshold rituals, household protection, Bagua symbolism, and Feng Shui. They are foundational for understanding protective practice as spatial and symbolic.
