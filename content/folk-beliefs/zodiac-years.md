# Overview

Zodiac years are years associated with the twelve Chinese Zodiac animals.

The animal year is one of the most familiar ways people encounter the Chinese Zodiac.

This page explains zodiac years as calendar symbolism and folk tradition.

It does not treat birth years as factual predictors of personality or fortune.

# Relationship to the Hub

`zodiac-years` is a core support page for `chinese-zodiac`.

It also strongly connects with `twelve-zodiac-animals` and `sexagenary-cycle`.

Together these pages explain how animal signs are placed within time.

# Animal Cycle

The twelve animals repeat in a regular sequence.

This sequence is explained in `twelve-zodiac-animals`.

The animal year gives a visible identity to a calendar year.

# Earthly Branches

Each animal corresponds to one of the `earthly-branches`.

This is the structural basis behind animal years.

The branch system gives zodiac years a place in traditional timekeeping.

# Stems and the Sixty-Year Cycle

Zodiac years are also part of the `sexagenary-cycle`.

The cycle combines `heavenly-stems` with Earthly Branches.

This creates a sixty-year sequence more detailed than the animal cycle alone.

# Five Elements

When stems are interpreted through the Five Elements, animal years may also receive elemental associations.

This topic belongs to `five-elements-and-zodiac`.

The existing `five-elements` page explains the symbolic background.

# Lunar Calendar Context

Zodiac years are tied to traditional calendar reckoning.

The `lunar-calendar` page explains how festival and year cycles are framed.
