# Overview

Ghost Month is a traditional period in Chinese folk belief associated with spirits, ancestors, offerings, and ritual caution. It is commonly linked to the seventh month of the lunar calendar.

# Cultural Background

In many Chinese communities, Ghost Month is believed to be a time when spirits are more active in the human world. Families may prepare offerings, burn incense, and observe certain taboos.

# Common Taboos

Commonly mentioned Ghost Month taboos include:

- avoiding swimming at night
- avoiding whistling after dark
- avoiding major life events
- being cautious around funeral-related places
- showing respect to wandering spirits

# Ritual Practices

Traditional practices may include food offerings, incense burning, ancestor remembrance, and community rituals intended to honor spirits and maintain harmony.

# Symbolic Meaning

Ghost Month reflects a broader cultural relationship between the living, the dead, ancestors, and unseen spiritual forces.

# Modern Interpretation

Today, Ghost Month is understood in different ways: as religious practice, folk tradition, family remembrance, seasonal taboo, or cultural heritage.
