# Overview

The Twelve Zodiac Animals are the most recognizable part of the Chinese Zodiac.

They form a repeating sequence of animal signs used in calendar symbolism and folk tradition.

The usual sequence is Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig.

This article treats the animals as cultural symbols.

It does not present animal signs as factual descriptions of personality or destiny.

# Animal Sequence

The animal sequence gives the zodiac its popular form.

Children, families, festivals, and New Year decorations often remember the cycle through animals.

The animals make an abstract calendar system easier to visualize.

The animals carry symbolic associations in folklore and popular interpretation.

These meanings vary by context and should not be treated as fixed facts.
