# Overview

Folk charms are small objects, written signs, images, knots, pendants, papers, or tokens used in customary symbolic practice.

They may be associated with protection, luck, health symbolism, household order, or festival renewal.

This archive treats folk charms as cultural artifacts.

It does not present charm effects as factual or guaranteed.

# Folk Magic Context

Folk charms belong to the wider topic of `folk-magic`.

They overlap with `protective-amulets`, `lucky-symbols`, and `ritual-objects`.

# Protective Forms

Some charms are carried, worn, posted, tied, folded, or placed near a doorway.

Related pages include `protective-charms`, `protective-talismans`, and `household-protection`.

# Images and Writing

Charms may include deity images, animals, characters, colors, or diagrams.

`deity-symbolism` and `guardian-symbols` explain some visual traditions.

# Festival Use

Charms may be renewed, exchanged, or displayed during festivals.

`festival-offerings` gives context for ritual material used during calendar observances.

# Zodiac Imagery

Animal signs and birth-year motifs may appear on popular charms.

This connects the topic to `chinese-zodiac`.

# Taboos and Purification

Charms may be used alongside `folk-taboos` and `ritual-purification`.

These practices express cultural ideas of boundary and order.
