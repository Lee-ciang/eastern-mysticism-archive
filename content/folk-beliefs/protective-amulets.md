# Overview

Protective amulets are portable objects associated with symbolic protection in folk traditions.

They may be worn, carried, hung, placed near children, or kept in a household space.

This archive treats amulets as cultural and historical objects.

It does not present protection claims as factual guarantees.

# Materials

Amulets may be made from metal, wood, stone, cloth, paper, thread, or other materials.

Material choice can carry family, regional, or symbolic meaning.
