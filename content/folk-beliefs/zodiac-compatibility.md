# Overview

Zodiac compatibility refers to traditional ideas about harmony, tension, or suitability between Chinese Zodiac signs.

These ideas appear in folk belief, marriage discussions, family customs, almanacs, and popular culture.

This archive treats compatibility as cultural symbolism.

It does not present compatibility rules as factual measures of relationships.

# Folk Belief Context

Compatibility ideas belong to `astrology-in-folk-beliefs`.

They show how calendar signs can become part of social interpretation.

Families may discuss zodiac signs in connection with marriage, birth years, or household harmony.

# Zodiac Animals

Compatibility is usually discussed through the animal signs.

This connects the topic to `twelve-zodiac-animals`.

The meanings of the animals are explored further in `zodiac-symbolism`.

# Zodiac Years

Because animal signs are associated with years, compatibility often begins with birth-year signs.

`zodiac-years` explains this year-based structure.

The archive presents this as inherited cultural practice rather than personal destiny.
