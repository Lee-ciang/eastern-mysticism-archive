# Overview

Astrology in folk beliefs refers to traditional ways of connecting calendar signs, zodiac animals, stems, branches, elements, and symbolic time with everyday cultural interpretation.

In this archive, astrology is treated as folklore and cultural history.

It is not presented as a factual system for prediction, medicine, gambling, finance, or life decisions.

# Folk Belief Setting

Zodiac ideas appear in family conversations, New Year customs, almanacs, marriage talk, birth-year references, and festival culture.

This page strongly connects with `lunar-calendar`, `traditional-festival-cycle`, `festival-offerings`, and `seasonal-rituals`.

Those pages show how calendar belief becomes part of social and ritual life.
