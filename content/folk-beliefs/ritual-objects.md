# Overview

Ritual objects are material items used in ceremonies, offerings, household customs, protective practices, and folk religious observance.

They may include incense burners, bowls, lamps, papers, charms, bells, images, containers, and altar furnishings.

This archive treats ritual objects as material culture.

It does not present objects as inherently supernatural.

# Meaning Through Context

An object's ritual meaning depends on use, setting, timing, and community tradition.

The same item may be ordinary in one setting and ceremonial in another.

# Offerings

Many ritual objects support offerings through bowls, trays, incense holders, lamps, or containers.

`festival-offerings` gives related context.

# Charms and Amulets

`folk-charms` and `protective-amulets` are portable forms of ritual material.

They connect with `protective-charms` and `protective-talismans`.

# Household Use

Ritual objects may be placed on altars, near doors, or in kitchens and family spaces.

This links the topic to `household-protection`.

# Deity Images

Images, tablets, and attributes may represent religious figures.

`deity-symbolism` and `guardian-symbols` explain visual meanings.

# Purification

Some objects are used in `ritual-purification` through water, incense, sound, or symbolic gestures.
