# Overview

Lucky symbols are images, words, colors, numbers, plants, animals, and objects associated with auspicious meaning.

They appear in gifts, festivals, homes, clothing, architecture, art, and commercial decoration.

This archive treats luck symbolism as cultural tradition.

It does not promise outcomes or financial gain.

# Auspicious Language

Some symbols depend on wordplay, sound resemblance, visual association, or inherited stories.

Meanings can vary by language, region, and historical period.
