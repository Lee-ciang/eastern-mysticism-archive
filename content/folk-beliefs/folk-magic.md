# Overview

Folk magic refers to customary practices involving charms, amulets, ritual objects, taboos, blessings, and symbolic protection.

In Chinese cultural settings, such practices may overlap with household religion, festival custom, ancestor belief, temple culture, and Taoist symbolism.

This page is the hub for the Folk Magic Cluster.

It treats magical traditions as cultural, historical, ritual, and symbolic practices.

It does not present supernatural claims as factual or guaranteed.

# Charms and Amulets

`folk-charms` examines small symbolic objects and written forms used in popular custom.

`protective-amulets` focuses on wearable or portable protective objects.

Existing authority pages include `protective-talismans` and `protective-charms`.

# Lucky Symbols

`lucky-symbols` covers images, colors, words, numbers, and motifs associated with good fortune.

These ideas often appear in festivals, households, gifts, and zodiac imagery.

# Taboos

`folk-taboos` explains customs that discourage certain actions, words, times, or placements.

Taboos can express social caution, ritual respect, or inherited folklore.

# Ritual Objects

`ritual-objects` introduces material items used in offerings, purification, protection, and household observance.

Their meaning depends on context rather than the object alone.
