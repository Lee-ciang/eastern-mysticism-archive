# Overview

Folk taboos are customary restrictions concerning actions, words, times, objects, directions, or social situations.

They can appear in festivals, funerals, weddings, childbirth customs, household life, and spirit beliefs.

This archive treats taboos as cultural and historical traditions.

It does not present feared consequences as factual certainty.

# Social Function

Taboos may express respect, caution, hierarchy, grief, cleanliness, or communal identity.

Some preserve practical concerns through symbolic language.

# Festival Taboos

Certain festivals include restrictions on speech, cleaning, travel, food, or behavior.

`festival-offerings` provides related ritual context.

# Household Taboos

Domestic taboos may concern doors, kitchens, altars, sleeping areas, or object placement.

This connects the topic to `household-protection`.

# Charms and Protection

Taboos may be paired with `folk-charms`, `protective-amulets`, `protective-charms`, and `protective-talismans`.
