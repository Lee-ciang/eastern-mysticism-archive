# Overview

Zodiac symbolism refers to the meanings, images, stories, and associations attached to Chinese Zodiac signs.

The animals of the Zodiac are not only calendar markers.

They are cultural symbols used in festivals, art, family speech, New Year imagery, and folk belief.

This page presents those meanings historically and neutrally.

# Animal Imagery

The `twelve-zodiac-animals` page explains the full animal sequence.

`zodiac-symbolism` focuses on how those animals become meaningful images.

Animals may suggest virtues, tendencies, warnings, humor, prosperity, or social identity.

# Festival and New Year Use

Zodiac animals are especially visible around the New Year season.

This links the page to `lunar-calendar` and `traditional-festival-cycle`.

Animal images may appear on decorations, gifts, paper cuttings, lanterns, and family greetings.

Zodiac symbolism may appear near almanacs and divination culture.

This connects it with `i-ching-divination` and `bagua`.

The systems are related historically but should not be treated as identical.
