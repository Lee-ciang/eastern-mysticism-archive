# Overview

Five Elements and Bagua refers to the relationship between Wu Xing, the Five Phases, and the Bagua, the Eight Trigrams. In Feng Shui and Chinese cosmology, these systems are often layered together to interpret space, direction, movement, and symbolic quality.

The Five Elements describe phases of transformation. The Bagua provides a diagrammatic structure of trigrams and directions.

# Historical Context

Both the Five Elements and Bagua developed within broader Chinese correlative thought. Over time, they became central to Feng Shui, divination, cosmology, ritual diagrams, and symbolic interpretation.

The relationship between them is not one single chart used everywhere. Different schools and historical systems developed different correspondences.

# Bagua Structure

The Bagua is made of eight trigrams. Each trigram combines yin and yang lines and is associated with natural images such as Heaven, Earth, Thunder, Wind, Water, Fire, Mountain, and Lake.

In Feng Shui, these trigrams may be arranged by direction and applied to spatial interpretation.

# Five Elements Structure

The Five Elements are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. They represent phases of change and relationship. Their generating and overcoming cycles describe support and regulation.

When combined with Bagua, the Five Elements add another layer of meaning to directions and spatial zones.

# Feng Shui Use

In Feng Shui, a Bagua map may be interpreted through elemental associations. A direction or trigram may be connected with Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water. This can influence how colors, forms, materials, or symbolic qualities are discussed.

The archive presents this as a traditional interpretive system rather than a guaranteed method for producing outcomes.

# Relationship to Qi Flow

Qi flow is often interpreted through both Bagua and Five Elements. The Bagua structures spatial meaning, while the Five Elements describe qualities of transformation. Qi provides the language of movement within that structure.

This relationship is one of the strongest bridges between symbolic cosmology and applied Feng Shui.

# Relationship to Yin Yang

Both systems depend on Yin Yang. The Bagua is built from yin and yang lines, and the Five Elements can be interpreted through yin-yang qualities such as rising and descending, active and receptive, warming and cooling.
