# Overview

The Luopan is a traditional Chinese geomantic compass used in Feng Shui practice. It contains multiple concentric rings filled with cosmological, directional, and symbolic information.

# Historical Origins

The Luopan developed from ancient Chinese magnetic compass traditions and became central to Feng Shui schools focused on spatial harmony and energy alignment.

# Symbolic Structure

A traditional Luopan contains layers associated with:
- Bagua trigrams
- Heavenly stems
- Earthly branches
- Five Elements
- Directional energies

# Feng Shui Application

Practitioners use the Luopan to analyze:
- building orientation
- energetic flow
- auspicious directions
- environmental harmony

# Cultural Significance

The Luopan remains one of the most recognizable tools associated with classical Chinese Feng Shui traditions.
