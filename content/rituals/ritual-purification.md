# Overview

Ritual purification refers to practices used to symbolically cleanse a person, object, space, threshold, altar, or ritual environment. In Chinese folk religion and Taoist ritual culture, purification may involve incense, water, fire, sound, sweeping, talismans, offerings, or formal liturgy.

The archive treats purification as cultural and religious practice, not as evidence of literal invisible contamination.

# Historical Context

Purification appears in many religious traditions. In Chinese contexts, it is connected with Taoist ritual preparation, temple practice, household renewal, funerary rites, seasonal festivals, and protective customs.

Purification may be performed before a ritual, after a disruption, during a transition, or as part of seasonal cleansing.

# Symbolic Meaning

Purification marks a change in state. It separates ordinary space from ritual space, disorder from order, or old conditions from renewed ones.

The meaning is often symbolic and social as well as religious. Cleaning a home before a festival, for example, can express renewal and readiness.

# Incense and Smoke

Incense is commonly associated with purification. Smoke may mark sacred presence, carry fragrance, and transform the atmosphere of a place.

In ritual interpretation, smoke can symbolize communication, offering, and cleansing. The archive describes this as traditional meaning.

# Fire and Talismans

Talisman burning may also function as purification in some contexts. Fire transforms paper into smoke and ash, making change visible.

This connects purification to talismanic practice and protection ritual.

# Water and Cleansing

Water may be used symbolically for washing, sprinkling, or marking renewal. In some traditions, purified or ritually prepared water appears in temple and household contexts.

Exact practices vary widely by region and lineage.

# Relationship to Warding

Purification and warding often overlap. Purification clears or prepares a space, while warding marks boundaries and resists harmful influence. Many rituals do both.

# Cultural Significance

Ritual purification is a major bridge between protection ritual, incense offering, talisman burning, household protection, threshold rituals, and qi-related ideas of movement and atmosphere.
