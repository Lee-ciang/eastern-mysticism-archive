# Overview

Li Tieguai is one of the Eight Immortals and is often called Iron Crutch Li.

He is commonly represented with an iron crutch and a gourd.

His legends explore transformation, appearance, compassion, and spiritual identity.

This archive treats these accounts as folklore and religious narrative.

# Eight Immortals Context

Li Tieguai belongs to the group described in `eight-immortals`.

His stories are part of `immortal-legends` and popular visual culture.

# Iron Crutch

The crutch is his most recognizable attribute.

It gives the figure a distinctive appearance in paintings, sculpture, and prints.

# Gourd Symbolism

Li Tieguai often carries a gourd.

In legend, the gourd may be associated with medicine, spirits, vapor, or hidden power.

The archive does not present medical or supernatural claims as fact.

# Appearance and Identity

Some stories contrast Li Tieguai's rough appearance with spiritual attainment.

This creates a moral theme about judging by outward form.

# Compassion Themes

Legends may connect the figure with care for the poor, sick, or marginalized.

These stories express cultural ideals rather than verified biography.
