# Overview

He Xiangu is one of the Eight Immortals and is commonly represented as a woman carrying a lotus or floral emblem.

Her stories appear in Taoist folklore, popular religion, art, and theater.

This archive treats He Xiangu as a legendary and religious figure.

It does not present immortal stories as factual biography.
