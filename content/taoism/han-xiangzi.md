# Overview

Han Xiangzi is one of the Eight Immortals and is commonly represented with a flute.

He appears in Taoist folklore, literary narratives, theater, and religious art.

His image connects immortality stories with music, nature, and cultivated refinement.

This archive treats Han Xiangzi as a legendary and cultural figure.

# Eight Immortals Context

Han Xiangzi belongs to `eight-immortals` and the narrative world of `immortal-legends`.

His flute distinguishes him in group imagery.

# Flute Symbolism

The flute may symbolize harmony, cultivated art, breath, or communication with nature.

These meanings are symbolic rather than evidence of supernatural music.

# Literary Associations

Some traditions connect Han Xiangzi with the scholar Han Yu.

The historical and legendary relationships should be distinguished carefully.

# Nature and Cultivation

Stories may place Han Xiangzi in landscapes, gardens, or mountain settings.

This connects the figure to `sacred-mountains` and Daoist natural imagery.
