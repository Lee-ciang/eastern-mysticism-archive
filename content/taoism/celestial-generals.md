# Overview

Celestial generals are divine martial figures associated with protection, command, ritual authority, and heavenly order in Taoist and Chinese religious imagination.

They may appear in ritual texts, temple imagery, talismans, protective rites, and stories of divine armies.

This page treats celestial generals as symbolic and religious figures.

It does not present them as factual military beings.
