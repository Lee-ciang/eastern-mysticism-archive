# Overview

Lu Dongbin is one of the best-known members of the Eight Immortals.

He appears in Taoist legend, literature, temple culture, drama, and popular art.

He is often represented as a scholar carrying a sword.

This archive treats Lu Dongbin as a legendary and religious figure.

It does not present immortal stories as factual biography.

# Eight Immortals Context

This page connects strongly to `eight-immortals` and `immortal-legends`.

Lu Dongbin often plays a prominent role in group stories and visual representations.

# Zhongli Quan

Some traditions connect Lu Dongbin with `zhongli-quan` as teacher and disciple.

Versions of this relationship vary across texts and legends.

# Sword Symbolism

Lu Dongbin's sword may symbolize discernment, spiritual discipline, or the cutting away of illusion.

It is a visual attribute rather than evidence of supernatural action.

# Scholar Identity

He is commonly shown in scholarly clothing.

This connects his image with learning, cultivation, and literary culture.
