# Overview

Thunder God refers to divine figures associated with thunder, command, protection, punishment, and ritual force in Chinese religious culture.

In Taoist settings, thunder deities may appear in thunder rites and protective ritual systems.

This article treats thunder deity traditions as cultural and religious symbolism.

It does not present thunder gods as factual meteorological causes.
