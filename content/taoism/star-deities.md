# Overview

Star deities are divine or sacred figures associated with stars, constellations, celestial order, and astral symbolism in Chinese religious culture.

They may appear in Taoist cosmology, temple rites, astrology, almanacs, and ritual texts.

This archive treats star deity traditions as religious and symbolic culture.

It does not present astrology or star deities as factual prediction systems.
