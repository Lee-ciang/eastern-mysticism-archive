# Overview

Lan Caihe is one of the Eight Immortals and is often shown carrying a flower basket.

The figure appears in folklore, theater, paintings, and popular religious art.

Stories differ about Lan Caihe's gender, age, dress, and social identity.

This archive treats those variations as part of literary and cultural tradition.

# Flower Basket

The flower basket is Lan Caihe's most recognizable attribute.

It may symbolize natural abundance, transience, beauty, or freedom from convention.

# Social Marginality

Some legends portray Lan Caihe as a wandering singer, beggar, eccentric, or performer.

These images place the figure outside ordinary social expectations.

# Gender Ambiguity

Lan Caihe has been represented in varied gendered forms.

This diversity should be documented without forcing one modern category onto every source.

# Nature and Wandering

The figure's stories often emphasize movement, flowers, music, and freedom.

These themes connect to `dao` and symbolic naturalness.
