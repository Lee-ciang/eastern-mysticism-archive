# Overview

The Wealth God, often called Caishen, is a figure associated with prosperity, good fortune, and festive blessing in Chinese folk religion.

This archive treats the Wealth God as a cultural and religious symbol.

It does not present worship of the Wealth God as a factual way to gain money.

It does not offer financial advice.
