# Overview

Deity offerings are ritual gifts or acts presented to deities in Chinese folk religion, Taoist temples, household worship, and festival contexts.

Offerings may include incense, fruit, tea, food, flowers, lamps, paper goods, or symbolic objects.

This article treats offerings as cultural and religious practices.

It does not present offerings as guaranteed ways to obtain supernatural results.

# Deities and Offerings

Offerings may be directed toward figures such as the `jade-emperor`, household deities, star deities, thunder figures, immortals, or local gods.

The wider cluster is organized through `chinese-deities` and `taoist-deities`.

# Temple Practice

Temple offerings often express reverence, gratitude, petition, remembrance, or participation in communal ritual.

The archive treats these meanings as religious and cultural traditions.

Regional practice varies widely.
