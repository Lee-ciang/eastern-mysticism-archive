# Overview

Zhang Guolao is one of the Eight Immortals and is often represented as an elderly figure riding a donkey backward.

He may carry a bamboo tube drum or musical instrument.

His stories emphasize eccentricity, age, reversal, and freedom from ordinary expectations.

This archive treats Zhang Guolao as a legendary and cultural figure.

# Eight Immortals Context

Zhang Guolao belongs to `eight-immortals` and appears throughout `immortal-legends`.

# Backward Donkey

Riding backward is one of his best-known visual motifs.

It may symbolize reversal, eccentric wisdom, or freedom from ordinary convention.

# Donkey Symbolism

The donkey is part of folklore rather than a claim about supernatural travel.

Some stories describe the animal in impossible or magical ways.

The archive presents these as narrative motifs.

# Bamboo Instrument

Zhang Guolao may carry a bamboo drum or tube.

This links his image with music, performance, and ritual sound.

# Age and Longevity

The figure's aged appearance connects him with longevity symbolism.

No medical or literal immortality claims are made.
